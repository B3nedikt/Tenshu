Tenshu Home shows the smart-home devices that a Tenshu Hub in your realm reports, and runs your automations on your phone. Your home data is encrypted and in sync across your devices.

TENSHU HOME NEEDS TENSHU CONTROL AND A TENSHU HUB
Tenshu Control holds your keys and connects your devices. Install Tenshu Control from Google Play and set it up first, then open Home. Home finds Control on its own.
To see and switch devices, Home needs a Tenshu Hub: a home server you run yourself, today an early, self-built setup for Zigbee devices. Without a Hub, the device list stays empty.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- See your devices by room, switch them on and off, and dim the lights that support it.
- Pair a new Zigbee device through your Hub.
- Create automations that run at a time of day, or at sunrise or sunset, with conditions such as a time window, a device's state or whether someone is home. Turn devices on or off, or run another automation.
- Switch automations on and off, and see in Activity when each one ran.

Home is early. Some options in the automation editor are not available yet, and the app says so. Settings for rooms, notifications and the Hub come later.

HOW HOME KEEPS YOUR HOME PRIVATE
- Home has no internet access of its own: it reaches your Hub and your other devices only through Tenshu Control.
- Your devices, automations and their activity are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Tenshu's servers store only encrypted data, and they don't have the keys to read it.
- The server can still see some facts it needs to sync: how much you store, how many devices you have, and when they sync.

REQUIREMENTS
- Android 7.0 or later.
- Tenshu Control, set up on the same phone.
- A Tenshu Hub, to see and switch devices.
- To sync between devices: a storage plan. The free plan has 5 GB.

Tenshu is open source and still in testing: expect rough edges, and tell us what breaks.
