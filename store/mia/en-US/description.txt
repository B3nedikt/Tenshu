Tenshu Mia answers questions about your own Tenshu data: your photos, files, places, workouts and calendar. It shows you what it found.

TENSHU MIA NEEDS TENSHU CONTROL
Tenshu Control holds your keys and connects your devices. Install Tenshu Control from the App Store and set it up first, then open Mia. Mia finds Control on its own.
Mia answers from the data your other Tenshu apps keep, so install the apps you want to ask about.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Type a question about your photos, files, places, workouts or calendar, and see what Mia found.
- Read your earlier questions and answers again in History, until you close Mia.

Mia is an early preview. It understands simple questions in English, and there is no AI model behind it yet. It only reads your data and never changes anything. On a new device there is little to find, so most answers say that nothing matched.

HOW MIA KEEPS YOUR QUESTIONS PRIVATE
- Your questions are answered on your iPhone or iPad, and are not sent to a server or an AI service.
- Mia has no voice input and does not use the microphone.
- Your chats are not saved: they are gone when you close Mia.

REQUIREMENTS
- iOS 16 or later, on iPhone or iPad.
- Tenshu Control, set up on the same device.
