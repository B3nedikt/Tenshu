Tenshu Drive needs Tenshu Control (app.tenshu.control.ios) installed and set up on the same device first. Control holds the encryption keys; Drive cannot open without it.

To review:
1. Install Tenshu Control and open it. Tap "Start fresh on this device", then "Continue", then "I've saved it".
2. Open Tenshu Drive. It finds Control on its own and shows an empty file list.
3. Tap "+" to upload a file or a photo, or to create a folder. Tap a file to open it; a file's actions include Rename, Move and Share Link.

Without "Turn on sync" in Control, files stay on the device and Drive shows that it is not syncing; that is expected. To try sync, use "Turn on sync" in Control: it asks for an email address and then a 6-digit code. Use the sign-in details in App Review Information: enter the user name as the email address and the password as the code (no email is sent to that address).

All files are end-to-end encrypted on the device before they reach our servers; the servers cannot read them.
