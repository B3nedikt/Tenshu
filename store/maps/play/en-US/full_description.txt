Tenshu Maps shows your places on a map that is drawn on your phone. Maps never uses your location.

TENSHU MAPS NEEDS TENSHU CONTROL
Tenshu Control holds your keys and connects your devices. Install Tenshu Control from Google Play and set it up first, then open Maps. Maps finds Control on its own.
Your places come from Tenshu Timeline: the places it records appear in Maps, on this phone and your other devices.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- See your places in a list, and open one to see it on the map.
- Download the map of a region to keep it on your phone, and delete it when you no longer need it.
- Keep the region around a place offline with one tap on the place's page.

Maps is early: it has no search and no directions yet.

HOW MAPS KEEPS YOUR PLACES PRIVATE
- Maps never uses your location.
- The map is drawn on your phone. Where you have not downloaded a region, Tenshu Control fetches the map around the place you open from Tenshu's map server, so that server can see which area you look at. Download a region, and its map comes from your phone.
- Your places are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Tenshu's servers store only encrypted data, and they don't have the keys to read it.
- The server can still see some facts it needs to sync: how much you store, how many devices you have, and when they sync.

REQUIREMENTS
- Android 7.0 or later.
- Tenshu Control, set up on the same phone.
- Tenshu Timeline, to record the places Maps shows.
- To sync between devices: a storage plan. The free plan has 5 GB.

Tenshu is open source and still in testing: expect rough edges, and tell us what breaks.
