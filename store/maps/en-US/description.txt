Tenshu Maps shows your places on a map that is drawn on your device, from map regions you download and keep. Maps never uses your location.

TENSHU MAPS NEEDS TENSHU CONTROL
Tenshu Control holds your keys and connects your devices. Install Tenshu Control from the App Store and set it up first, then open Maps. Maps finds Control on its own.
Your places come from Tenshu Timeline: the places it records appear in Maps, on this device and your others.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- See your places in a list, and open one to see it on the map.
- Download the map of a region to keep it on your device, and delete it when you no longer need it. Maps shows the map only where you have downloaded a region.
- Use Apple Maps instead, if you prefer. It is off until you turn it on.

HOW MAPS KEEPS YOUR PLACES PRIVATE
- Maps never uses your location.
- The map is drawn on your device from the regions you download. Tenshu's map server sees which regions you download, not which places you look at.
- Your places are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Tenshu's servers store only encrypted data, and they don't have the keys to read it.
- The server can still see some facts it needs to sync: how much you store, how many devices you have, and when they sync.
- If you turn on Use Apple Maps, Apple serves the map, and sees which place you open and when.

REQUIREMENTS
- iOS 16 or later, on iPhone or iPad.
- Tenshu Control, set up on the same device.
- Tenshu Timeline, to record the places Maps shows.
- To sync between devices: a storage plan. The free plan has 5 GB.
