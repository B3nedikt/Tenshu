Tenshu Fit records your workouts and brings in your activity from Apple Health, encrypted and in sync across your devices. Only your own devices can read it.

TENSHU FIT NEEDS TENSHU CONTROL
Tenshu Control holds your keys and connects your devices. Install Tenshu Control from the App Store and set it up first, then open Fit. Fit finds Control on its own.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Record a run, walk, ride or strength workout. See time, distance, pace and steps as you go, with the route from GPS.
- Connect a Bluetooth heart rate strap to see your heart rate while you record.
- Add the workouts you record to Apple Health.
- Import the last 30 days from Apple Health, if you allow it: workouts, steps, distance, active energy, sleep, weight and height, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, food energy, water and cycle data. Fit imports again when you open it.
- Import workouts from a Google Takeout .zip, or from .gpx and .tcx files.
- Look back over your workouts, each with its route, laps and heart rate, and see simple trends over time.
- Set a daily goal for active minutes.
- Use Apple Maps for workout maps, if you prefer. It is off until you turn it on.

Fit is for keeping track of your activity. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.

HOW FIT KEEPS YOUR DATA PRIVATE
- Your workouts and health data are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Tenshu's servers store only encrypted data, and they don't have the keys to read it.
- The server can still see some facts it needs to sync: how much you store, how many devices you have, and when they sync.
- Fit has no ads and no analytics, and it does not share your health data with anyone.
- Fit uses your location only while you record a workout.
- If you turn on Use Apple Maps, Apple serves the map, and sees which workout you open and when.
- Without a storage plan, your data stays on this device.

REQUIREMENTS
- iOS 16 or later, on iPhone. On iPad, Apple Health needs iPadOS 17 or later.
- Tenshu Control, set up on the same device.
- To sync between devices: a storage plan. The free plan has 5 GB.
