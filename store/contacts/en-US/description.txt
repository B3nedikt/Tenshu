Tenshu Contacts shows the people in your Tenshu realm, encrypted and in sync across your devices. Only your own devices can read them.

TENSHU CONTACTS NEEDS TENSHU CONTROL
Tenshu Control holds your keys and connects your devices. Install Tenshu Control from the App Store and set it up first, then open Contacts. Contacts finds Control on its own.
On iPhone and iPad, Contacts shows the contacts you added in Tenshu Contacts on your other devices, and the people who accepted your invitation. You cannot add or edit a contact on iPhone or iPad yet.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Find a contact by name, phone number, email address or organization.
- See a contact's phone numbers, email addresses, organization and notes.
- Verify a person you connected with: compare a six-digit code with them, and see a warning when their key changes.
- Find possible duplicates, choose the one to keep, and merge them.
- Set the name the other Tenshu apps show for you.

HOW CONTACTS KEEPS YOUR CONTACTS PRIVATE
- Your contacts are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded. Tenshu's servers store only encrypted data, and they don't have the keys to read it.
- The server can still see some facts it needs to sync: how much you store, how many devices you have, and when they sync.
- Tenshu has no directory, so nobody can look you up. You connect with someone only when you both accept an invitation.
- Contacts does not read the address book on your device.
- Without a storage plan, your contacts stay on this device.

REQUIREMENTS
- iOS 16 or later, on iPhone or iPad.
- Tenshu Control, set up on the same device.
- To sync between devices: a storage plan. The free plan has 5 GB.
