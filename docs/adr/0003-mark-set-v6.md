- **Owner:** Benedikt

## Superseded — read ADR 0004 instead

This ADR retired the cat mark's triangle "pagoda-eave" ears and parked the Hanko
variant. ADR 0004 reversed the ears decision, and **restates in full** the two
parts of this ADR that survived it — Hanko stays parked, and the Solid variant's
whiskers are drawn in `currentColor`. Nothing here is still authoritative.

The reasoning that produced the reversal is in ADR 0004's *Context*; the original
argument for removing the ears (they blended into the dome at 16–20px) is in this
file's history.

**This file is kept as a stub rather than deleted** because
[ADR 0023](0023-density-single-mode.md) cites it by number: two ADRs were once
filed as 0004, and the tiebreak recorded there is that the mark-set ADR is *"the
successor ADR 0003 names by number in its own supersession header"*. Removing
0003 would strand that rationale and leave a hole in the sequence.

For app icons — a different artefact — see
[ADR 0026](0026-app-icon-system.md).
