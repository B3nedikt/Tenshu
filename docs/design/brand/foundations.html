01Mark

A watchful cat, drawn two ways.

Tenshu (天守) is the keep of a Japanese castle — the watchtower at the center. The marks lean into that: a sketch cat with round eyes, a soft ω mouth and outward-curving whiskers; and the same cat filled in. Cute first, watchful second. Use the outline whenever possible — the solid is reserved for app icons, favicons, and reversed chrome. (A hanko-seal variant is parked for a later phase.)

A · Outline — primary

The watchful house cat.

A cleaned-up version of the original drawing. Half-dome head, round dot eyes with a tiny catchlight, kaomoji ω mouth, fanning whiskers. Used everywhere unless there's a reason not to.

48 · 32 · 20 · 16

B · Solid — secondary

Same cat, filled.

Filled head, eyes and mouth carved out as negative space. Whiskers stay outlined — they emerge from the cheek and read like the outline mark. Reach for it on app icons, favicons, and reversed chrome where the outline would lose presence at small sizes.

48 · 32 · 20 · 16

Construction

The cat lives on a 64×64 grid with a 4u safe margin. Head: 36×30 squircle, rx=9, centered on x=32. Ears: equilateral isoceles triangles, peaks at y=6, bases on the head's top edge.

Clear space

Reserve at least 1× the cap-height of the wordmark on every side. On product chrome that means a minimum of 12px around a 24px mark.

Color

Always single-color in currentColor. The mark inherits the surrounding text. Never tint, gradient, or duotone.

Don't

  • Don't add ears — the dome is the cat now. Earlier triangle-ear drafts are retired.
  • Don't fill the whiskers white on the solid mark. They stay outlined, in currentColor, extending outward from the cheek.
  • Don't reach for Solid when Outline reads cleanly. Primary first.
  • Don't rotate or skew. The cat is upright by design.

03Color

Amethyst is canonical. Slate is the only secondary.

ADR-0001 commits to a muted violet — quiet, mature, faintly spiritual. Slate is kept as a hue-free fallback for data-dense surfaces and co-branded contexts (ADR-0002 retires Indigo and Jade). 08Imagery

Quiet by tier. Line first, photography sometimes, never both at once.

The brand is naturally restrained, so imagery has to earn its place. Two surfaces, two recipes — the third (in-app personalisation) waits for an M3-Expressive-shaped feature to land.

Tier A · Marketing Quiet

Line illustrations, with desaturated editorial photography where it earns its place.

  • Line. Single-weight strokes (1.5–2px) in --primary over --surface-2. No gradients, no fill flourishes.
  • Photo. Editorial only, desaturated to fit the Amethyst temperature — used sparingly, when it carries information line illustration can't (people, devices in context).
  • Mix rule. Pick one per surface. Line and photography never share a hero.
Tier B · Product Almost none

Minimal line illustrations only. Nothing decorative.

  • Used for. Empty states, error pages, onboarding step markers. Same stroke vocabulary as the icon set.
  • Most surfaces. Most product views are productive — no imagery at all. Layout, type and the cat carry the brand.
  • Future. When timeline-shaped views land (M3 Expressive, or user-photo personalisation), they'll adapt to the user — not via stock illustration. Tracked separately.
09Type — fallback

Geist over the network, the platform's own letter-form when not.

Geist is the brand voice and stays that on web. Native apps fall back to the platform's system font — it loads instantly, matches the user's other apps, and keeps Tenshu out of the network for typography. Pair these stacks per platform; don't ship Geist on native.

Web (Tenshu apex, Drive, Control)

Geist Sans / Mono

Primary. Loaded via Google Fonts with font-display: swap. The system stack underneath catches anyone offline.

font-family: 'Geist', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont,
             'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;
iOS · macOS

San Francisco

No webfont. Use the platform UI font directly — SF Pro Text / SF Pro Display via the standard Apple stack. SF Mono for monospace.

-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'SF Pro Text',
'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;
Android

Roboto / Roboto Flex

Native default — already on the device. Roboto Flex where opsz / weight axes are wanted, otherwise the static Roboto stack.

'Roboto Flex', 'Roboto', system-ui, sans-serif;
